### (b) The key correction: cadence is a strong covariate but **cannot be the whole fault**

10-23 corrected 10-21, demoting "two separable faults" and adopting *one fault, two estimators*
— median book age and maximum cadence both reading loop duration. Over 99 publishes that is
**half right, and the missing half is what matters operationally.**

Supporting it: cadence minimum and median book age are strongly rank-correlated, **Spearman
ρ = 0.844** across the 91 numeric publishes, and still **ρ = 0.723** inside a narrow cadence
band. They are not independent.

Against it being sufficient: **restrict to the 59 publishes whose cadence minimum lies in the
common 43–76 s band, and median book age still spans 2,279 → 47,720 ms — a 21x spread.** One
loop-duration fault seen through two estimators does not produce 21x variation at matched
cadence. The sharpest pair:

| publish | cadence min | cadence max | median book age |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10-23 11:57 | **265 s** | 853 s | **2,176 ms** |
| 10-25 08:05 | **43 s** | 204 s | **30,174 ms** |

The publish with ~6x slower cycles has ~14x **lower** book age. Both are ordinary publishes, not
`BLIND` and not outliers of the excursion kind.

What the 99 publishes actually show is a **level that moves on its own clock**, with cadence
modulating around it. The level resets abruptly and then grows: 10-22 05:53 → 06:53 drops
**29,274 → 470 ms in one hour** (62x), 10-22 20:54 → 21:55 drops **23,481 → 2,856 ms** (8x), and
after each reset the metric climbs back over many hours (470 → 23,481 ms across 14 h; 3,129 →
31,225 ms across 21 h). It is not purely uptime-driven — one post-reset stretch, 10-22 21:55 →
10-23 12:57, stayed flat at 1,566–4,013 ms for ~15 h without ramping. And the clearest
decoupling is the three consecutive publishes right after the 10-23 08:57–09:57 `BLIND`
episode: book age 2,279 / 2,176 / 3,097 ms while the cadence minimum sat at 48 / 265 / 57 s.
Recovery in the parse-to-signal path arrived **before** cadence recovered.

**Operationally, and this is the one thing to carry to the box:** because book age varies 21x at
matched cadence, fixing the cadence stall is **necessary but not sufficient**. 10-23 sent the box

abrupt drops and what accumulates afterwards in the **row-parse → signal-write** path

row count, work added per row, lost batching or concurrency, a growing buffer. Cadence alone
