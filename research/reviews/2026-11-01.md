The two failures are a **swap**: 15:35 was handed the 16:30 sample and 16:35 was handed a $36.96.

predicted hour read a normal −$0.14.

Reconciling all 25 deltas mechanically (implied reference = printed − delta, against the published
spot series, same hour previous day): **23 ok, 2 mismatched, 0 unresolvable.** Both mismatches are
the pair above. Every other delta in the window reconciles to the cent.

What survives of F1 and what breaks:

- **Survives (2/2):** each `$0.00` print produces exactly one phantom `+$36.8x` 24 h gain a day
  later. Two prints, two echoes (+36.89, +36.82). The harm statement stands in full — a silent

- **Breaks (1/2):** the 24-hour alignment, and with it 10-31's "reference leg is sound in 24/24".
  That 24/24 was over a window that did not contain these publishes, so it was not wrong then; it

**Consequence for q3, which is the only thing to act on.** Two readings fit, and this checkout
