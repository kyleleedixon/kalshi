### (c) The two statistics are not interchangeable — sharpening 10-23 §1(c)

Over the 46 publishes carrying a numeric read:

- corr(median book age, cadence **minimum**) = **+0.892**
- corr(median book age, cadence **maximum**) = **−0.149**

10-23's "one fault, two estimators" survives only in the weak sense that both derive from loop
duration. Operationally the cadence **maximum carries no information about staleness** — the median
tracks the typical cycle and is blind to exactly the outlier cycles the maximum is built to catch.
This window is the clean demonstration: 30 h with every cadence maximum ≤226 s while the median
climbed 2,691 → 31,225 ms.

The window also shows the shape, twice. It is a **sawtooth**: after a recovery the loop sits at
~33 s cadence / ~3,000 ms book age, degrades monotonically over roughly a day to ~60 s / ~30,000 ms,
plateaus there, and eventually stalls. Both recoveries are in reach (10-23 16:58 and 10-24 07:00);
the second degradation ran 10-24 07:00 (33-79 s, 2,691 ms) → 10-25 07:05 (60-116 s, 30,837 ms) and
has been flat at ~60 s / ~30,000 ms since. **That is the box target** — not the stalls, which cost
less and cleared themselves.
